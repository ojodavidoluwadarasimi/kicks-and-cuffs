Delivery

We deliver across Nigeria. You choose your state at checkout, and your delivery fee is shown before you pay.

You place your order, pay with Paystack, and your payment is confirmed automatically. You get an order number and a tracking page that shows each step: packing, on the way and delivered.

Delivery times count working days from the day your payment is confirmed. Orders are packed as soon as your payment is confirmed.

Returns and exchanges

A shoe that does not fit is no fun. If your pair is not right, you can return or exchange it within days of delivery.

  • The shoes must be unworn, clean and in the original box.
  • Exchanges for another size are free of charge, subject to stock.
  • If we sent the wrong pair or the shoes arrive damaged, tell us straight away and we will fix it at our cost.
  • We cannot take back shoes that have been worn outside, washed, or altered.

To start a return, message us with your order number and the reason. We will tell you how to send the pair back.

Size guide

Our sizes are European (EU). To find yours, stand on a sheet of paper, mark the longest toe and the back of your heel, and measure the gap in centimeters. Do this in the evening, when feet are at their widest.

Between two sizes? Go up one size in oxfords, derbies and loafers if you plan to wear thick socks. Sneakers are true to size. The Lagos Runner has a roomy toe, so go with your usual size.

The chart is a guide, and foot length is approximate. Every shoe page also shows live stock by size.

Payment

You place your order with your delivery address, and your pair is held for you. Then you pay securely with Paystack by card, bank transfer or USSD. The moment the payment goes through, your order page shows "Payment received" and we start packing. If an order is not paid within 6 hours, the pair goes back on the shelf.

Prefer to talk first? Use the WhatsApp button on your cart. It sends the seller your full order, so we can continue the chat there.

We only ask for the details we need to deliver your pair: your name, phone number, email and address.

About us

We sell two kinds of shoes: the ones you wear Monday to Friday and the ones you wear on the weekend. Oxfords, derbies, monk straps and loafers for the office. Court sneakers, runners, high tops and slip ons for everything else.

We built this store around one simple idea. When you want a pair of shoes, you should be able to pick your size, see what is actually in stock, know the full price before you pay, and follow your order until it arrives.

Every shoe page tells you what the shoe is, who it is for, how it fits and how to look after it, so you can choose with confidence.
